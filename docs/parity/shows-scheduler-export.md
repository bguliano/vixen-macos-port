# Shows, Scheduler, Export and other tools: parity

Status of every F number in `docs/spec/shows-scheduler-export.md`. **done**: built and looked at; **partial**: what's missing is said; **todo**: not started; **omitted**: why is said. Passes hand over through this table.

**For whoever tests in the app**: with the Administration area in the build, a script must open `administration` first, because that area boots the profile. The wizard is driven by the hook `wizard` (`app/Sources/VixenScheduler/SchedulerTestHooks.swift`). Shows and the scheduler are driven by the hooks `shows`, `showEditor` and `scheduler`; services don't start under the harness, so `["scheduler","drive","3"]` stands in for the scheduler's thread for three seconds.


> November 11 continuation: the Element Mapper now serves the sequencer’s unmapped-effects prompt; map assignment/save/application was exercised in the combined app on a synthetic sequence. Its initial clipped window was corrected and the final 800×600 layout inspected. Package import/export and Vixen 2 import are now implemented and verified; see their rows and `docs/VERIFICATION.md` for evidence and remaining manual checks.
